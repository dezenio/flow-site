Connecting Zoom

The full guide is Flow for Zoom.

  • In Flow, open Settings › Account and choose Connect Zoom. Sign in to Zoom in the window that opens and allow Flow.
  • Flow then reads your AI Companion summaries and My Notes for your meetings, and can add a Zoom link when you schedule a meeting in Flow.
  • Summaries missing? AI Companion meeting summaries must be turned on in your Zoom settings, and the meeting's host must start one.
  • Disconnect: choose Disconnect next to Zoom in Settings › Account, or remove Flow under Manage › Added Apps in your Zoom account.

Connecting Microsoft 365

  • Sign in with your work account when Flow asks. Some organizations require an administrator to approve Flow first; if you see "Need admin approval", ask your IT team.
  • Flow keeps its data in a Flow folder in your OneDrive, so your devices stay in step.

Your data

Flow runs on your devices and keeps your data there and in your own OneDrive; Dezenio doesn't receive it. See the Privacy Policy for what Flow reads, where it goes, and how to remove it.
