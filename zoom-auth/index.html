Finish connecting in Flow

Flow connects to Zoom from the app itself, on each of your devices:

  • Open Flow on your Mac, iPhone or iPad.
  • Go to Settings › Account and choose Connect Zoom.
  • Sign in to Zoom in the window that opens and allow Flow.

Flow then writes up your Zoom meetings from their AI Companion summaries and your My Notes, and can add a Zoom link when you schedule a meeting in Flow.
