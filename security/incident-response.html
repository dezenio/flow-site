Incident Management and Response Policy

Dezenio, LLC · Effective November 11, 2026 · Reviewed at least once a year

What counts as an incident

Any event that may affect the confidentiality, integrity or availability of Flow or its users' data: for example, a compromised developer account or signing key, a vulnerability being exploited, or a release that mishandles user data.

Response

  1. Contain: revoke affected credentials or keys (for example, rotating API keys, revoking certificates, or asking Zoom or Microsoft to disable an app registration), and stop affected releases.
  2. Assess: determine what happened, which users and data may be affected, and the cause.
  3. Fix: release a corrected version and verify it.
  4. Notify: tell affected users and, where they are involved, Zoom, Microsoft or Apple without undue delay, and as required by law.
  5. Learn: record the incident and follow-up actions in the issue log.

Contact

Report suspected incidents to support@dezenio.com. Reports are acknowledged within 3 business days, and sooner for suspected exposure of user data.
