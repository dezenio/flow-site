Flow

Infrastructure and Dependency Management Policy

Dezenio, LLC · Effective November 11, 2026 · Reviewed at least once a year

Infrastructure

  • Flow has no server infrastructure of its own. Its public web pages are static files served by GitHub Pages over HTTPS.
  • Third-party services Flow connects to (Microsoft Graph, Zoom, Anthropic) are accessed directly from the user's device with the user's own credentials.

Dependencies

  • Flow uses only Apple's system frameworks and includes no third-party packages or SDKs.
  • Adding any third-party dependency requires a review of its need, maintenance, license and security record; the version is pinned and monitored for advisories.
  • The development toolchain (Xcode and Apple SDKs) is updated to current releases within 30 days of release, or sooner for security fixes.
  • APIs used are reviewed when providers announce changes or deprecations.
