Flow for Zoom

How to add, use and remove the Flow app for Zoom

What it does

Flow is a work assistant for Mac, iPhone and iPad. With Zoom connected, after each meeting Flow reads the meeting's AI Companion summary and your My Notes and writes the meeting up for you: key points, decisions and the to-dos that fall to you. When you schedule a meeting in Flow, it can add a Zoom link.

Before you start

  • Flow installed on your Mac, iPhone or iPad.
  • A Zoom account. For summaries, turn on AI Companion meeting summaries in your Zoom settings; the meeting's host starts one during the meeting.
  • If your Zoom account is managed by your organization, an admin may need to allow Flow in the Zoom App Marketplace.

Adding Flow to Zoom

  1. Open Flow and go to Settings › Account.
  2. Choose Connect Zoom. A Zoom sign-in window opens.
  3. Sign in to Zoom and review what Flow asks for, then choose Allow.
  4. The window closes and Settings shows Zoom as connected.

Zoom allows one sign-in per app, so one of your devices reads Zoom and shares what it reads with your other devices through your Flow folder.

Using Flow with Zoom

  • Meeting write-ups: after a Zoom meeting ends and its AI Companion summary is ready, Flow adds the meeting's write-up and to-dos. Open the meeting in Flow to read them, edit them, or file them with a project.
  • My Notes: notes you take in Zoom's My Notes during a meeting are added to that meeting in Flow.
  • Zoom links: when you create a meeting in Flow, choose Zoom as the meeting link and Flow creates the Zoom meeting for you.

What Flow can access

Only your own Zoom data: your meetings and past meetings, their AI Companion summaries, your My Notes (with their transcripts) found through Zoom Docs search, and creating meetings you schedule in Flow. Requests go directly from your device to Zoom; Dezenio runs no servers and doesn't receive your data. See the Privacy Policy.

Removing Flow

  1. In Flow, go to Settings › Account and choose Disconnect Zoom. Flow deletes its Zoom sign-in from the device and stops reading from Zoom.
  2. In Zoom, sign in at marketplace.zoom.us, go to Manage › Added Apps, find Flow and choose Remove. This revokes Flow's access for all your devices.
  3. Meeting write-ups Flow already made stay in Flow until you delete them. To remove all of Flow's data, delete the app and the Flow folder in your OneDrive.

Troubleshooting

  • No write-up for a meeting: the meeting had no AI Companion summary or My Notes, or Zoom is still preparing it. Flow checks again later.
  • "Zoom didn't allow…" message: choose Reconnect Zoom in Settings › Account and allow everything Flow asks for.
